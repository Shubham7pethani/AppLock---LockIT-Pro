Privacy Policy for AppLock - LockIt Pro

Last Updated: May 17, 2026

Thank you for choosing to use AppLock - LockIt Pro ("we", "us", "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect and Use

AppLock - LockIt Pro is designed with privacy as a priority. All core functionalities are performed locally on your device. We require certain permissions to provide our services, which are explained below:

  • Usage Data Access: We require this permission to detect which application is currently running in the foreground. This allows us to instantly lock the apps you have selected to protect. We do not track, collect, or share your app usage history externally.
  • Display Over Other Apps (Overlay): This permission is necessary to draw the lock screen over the protected applications when they are launched.
  • Camera Access: We request camera access exclusively for the "Intruder Selfie" feature. If enabled, the app will take a photo of anyone who repeatedly enters the wrong password. These photos are saved strictly locally on your device's internal storage and are never transmitted to our servers or any third parties.
  • Notifications: We use notifications to ensure the app lock service remains active in the background and to alert you of unauthorized access attempts (Intruder Alerts).

2. Data Storage and Security

All data generated by the app, including your lock PIN, pattern, application preferences, and intruder selfies, are encrypted and stored locally on your device. We do not have servers that collect, store, or process your personal data.

3. Third-Party Services

Our application operates offline and does not integrate third-party analytics or tracking SDKs that collect your personal information. Your privacy is fully contained within your device.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at the email address provided on our Google Play Store listing.
